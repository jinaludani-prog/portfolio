01. About Me

Hey there! If you're reading this, you probably want to know a bit about the human behind the screen. I'm Jinal—a curious problem solver, an engineering student at GLS University, and someone who runs on equal parts ambition and a slightly concerning amount of coffee. ☕

I didn't just wake up knowing how to code. My journey started with breaking things, asking "why did that happen?", and spending hours reading documentation to put it back together. That curiosity led me to the intense, fast-paced world of hackathons. After competing in three consecutive Smart India Hackathons (2024, 2025, 2026) and the Tic Tech Toe–2026 National Hackathon, I learned something crucial: I love building things that actually matter.

Whether I'm sketching out a user flow in Figma, soldering pins onto an ESP32 microcontroller, or training an AI model to detect cost anomalies, my goal is always the same: make it useful, make it scalable, and make it beautiful.

When I step away from the keyboard, I'm usually reading up on AI philosophy (Alan Turing's early theories fascinate me!), exploring new music, or brainstorming my next big app idea.

03. Things I'm Proud Of

Tic Tech Toe 2026 | DAIICT

Cloud FinOps Intelligence

An AI-powered multi-cloud cost anomaly detection and spend forecasting system. Selected for the offline round, this platform utilizes Z-Score, Isolation Forests, and LSTM Autoencoders to detect spending deviations and "zombie" resources. This carbon-conscious optimization approach successfully achieved a ~10% reduction in cloud costs and ~24% reduction in carbon emissions.

  • AI/ML
  • LSTM Autoencoder
  • Cloud FinOps
  • Python

Capstone Project

Skincare Synergy

A hybrid neuro-symbolic platform analyzing ingredient interactions. Supervised by Dr. Aakanksha Jain, this system integrates Google Cloud Vision OCR, custom spaCy NER, and a Neo4j clinical knowledge graph, delivering insights via an on-device SLM with a Groq Cloud fallback.

  • Flutter
  • Python/FastAPI
  • Neo4j
  • AI/ML

SIH 2026 Innovation

SmartKheti

Building upon my experiences from previous hackathons, I developed an offline-capable IoT field station and companion app for comprehensive agricultural monitoring. It integrates an ESP32-S3 microcontroller with multiple sensors to track soil metrics locally.
